OUR ROOTS, OUR PROMISE

From a cherished tradition to your everyday table.

INNIYA is a new food-brand concept inspired by the warmth of Bihar, the joy of sharing prasad and the simple comfort of traditional Indian flavours. Our journey begins with Thekua — with a long-term dream of bringing more regional favourites to homes across India.

We believe good food should feel familiar, be presented thoughtfully and bring people closer together.
